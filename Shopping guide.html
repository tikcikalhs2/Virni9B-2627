���� GUIDA ALL'ACQUISTO (SHOPPING GUIDE)

Welcome to our authentic Italian culinary online store! To ensure the best shopping experience and that your fresh dishes arrive perfectly at your dining table, please follow these 6 easy shopping steps :

1. Browse the Homepage (Index)

  • Visit our Index to view our entire catalog of traditional Italian dishes.
  • You can explore the menu by category: Antipasto , Primo Piatto , Secondo Piatto , or our 4 premium Dolce variants.

2. Choose Your Favorite Dishes

  • Click on the photo or product name to view dish details, ingredients, and prices (in USD).
  • Select the quantity of servings you wish to order.
  • Click the "Add to Cart" button. You can return to the Index page if you want to add more dishes.

3. Review Your Shopping Cart

  • Once you have finished selecting, click the Shopping Cart icon in the top right corner of the website.
  • Double-check your list of dishes and quantities to ensure everything is correct.
  • If everything is correct, click the "Proceed to Checkout" button.

4. Fill In Shipping & Contact Details

  • Enter your full name, active WhatsApp number, and detailed shipping address (Street Name, House Number, District, and Postal Code).
  • Important Note: Since our products consist of fresh, traditional dishes without preservatives, please ensure your address is within range for instant or same-day delivery to maintain food quality.

5. Select Payment Method & Confirm

  • Choose your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Digital Wallet).
  • Complete the payment according to the total nominal amount stated on the invoice (including shipping fees).
  • Our system will verify the payment automatically, or you can upload your proof of transfer directly on the page.

6. Sit Back & Wait for Your Order!

  • Once payment is verified, our kitchen team will immediately prepare and pack your dishes hygienically using food-grade packaging.
  • You will receive a tracking number or a courier tracking link via WhatsApp/Email to monitor your Italian meal's journey right to your doorstep.
